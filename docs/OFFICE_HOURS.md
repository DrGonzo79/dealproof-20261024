# Office Hours product diagnostic

## Specific customer
Primary user: a self-funded first-time searcher who reviews 20–50 CIMs per acquisition and needs to decide whether to spend time, legal fees, and credibility on an LOI. Initial economic buyer: a boutique SBA lender branch or SMB broker that loses cycle time when bad financing math survives too long.

## Painful status quo
The broker's SDE is treated as truth, owner labor is inconsistently normalized, and addbacks are argued in spreadsheets without lineage. The buyer discovers after LOI that a manager must be hired or the lender supports less debt. That creates an equity gap after sunk time and fees.

## Desperate use case
“I have a $3.8M asking price, an $800K CIM number, and 48 hours to decide whether to offer. Show which addbacks survive, what replacement management costs, whether modeled DSCR clears 1.25x, and how much extra equity I may need.”

## Narrowest paid wedge
A human-reviewed, lender-readable pre-LOI earnings bridge for one $3M+ SBA acquisition: supported addbacks, replacement-cost estimate, DSCR sensitivity, and evidence questions. Charge $99 only after five target buyers complete the free screen and at least three ask to forward the output to a lender. Do not sell “AI QoE.”

## Observation / validation plan
Recruit 15 qualified searchers from Searchfunder/X and 5 lender or broker operators. Observe them use a redacted historical CIM. Measure: completion, which inputs require expert help, disagreement with the verdict, forward-to-lender intent, and explicit acceptance/rejection of $99. Success in 14 days: 8 completed screens, 5 forward intents, 3 explicit $99 yeses, and 2 partner calls. Kill/pivot if fewer than 3 complete without founder rescue or no lender trusts the bridge.

## Future-fit
If the report becomes credible, the durable product is lender/broker intake infrastructure and a proprietary reviewed adjustment corpus—not a generic searcher subscription. Human review, evidence provenance, model monitoring, and regulated-claim boundaries are product features.
