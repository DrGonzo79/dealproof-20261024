# Phased build prompts

1. **Secure intake:** “Add authenticated multi-tenant upload of redacted CIM PDFs, malware scanning, retention controls, consent, and page-level provenance. Keep manual entry available. Test cross-tenant access and deletion.”
2. **Extraction + review:** “Extract financial tables and addbacks into reviewable candidates with page citations and calibrated confidence. Never compute money in the model. Build a reviewer queue and measure precision, recall, overrides, and latency.”
3. **Scenario engine:** “Version SBA assumptions, debt terms, replacement benchmarks, and adjustment decisions. Produce deterministic sensitivity tables with golden tests and audit events.”
4. **Lender memo:** “Generate an accessible lender-readable PDF with evidence lineage, unresolved questions, disclaimers, ruleset version, and reviewer identity. Require explicit approval before export.”
5. **Partner pilot:** “Add broker/lender intake, role-based access, partner branding, reuse analytics, and outcome feedback. No autonomous approvals. Instrument completion, forward rate, paid conversion, override rate, and false-GO incidents.”
