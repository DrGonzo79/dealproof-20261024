# Research notes — IdeaBrowser Idea of the Day, October 24, 2026

Public source: https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/ideas/today

## Brief
- **Title:** Pre-offer screening tool that stress-tests financials for small business buyers
- **IB Index:** 7.0; B2B SaaS; reported ceiling $3M–$6M ARR.
- **Reality:** high difficulty, $5K–$20K starting capital, 60–120 days to first revenue. Year-one estimate $60K–$130K (the detailed model's base case is about $87K and is assumption-heavy).
- **Buyer/user:** user is a first-time or independent searcher pursuing sub-$5M businesses with SBA 7(a) financing. Better payer is an SBA lender branch or SMB broker shop.
- **Painful status quo:** a CIM's earnings include aggressive addbacks, owner labor is omitted, and the financing gap appears only after LOI and paid diligence. The report cites 21%+ of broken LOIs tied to financial discrepancies.
- **Wedge/gap:** the pre-LOI screening category already exists. The viable wedge is a lender-grade verdict: addback lineage, replacement costs, DSCR, and supported loan/equity gap—not generic CIM extraction.
- **Why now:** the report says SBA SOP 50 10 8.1 took effect October 1, 2026; for $3M+ acquisition purchase prices, an independent QoE earnings figure drives a 1.25x DSCR calculation, projections do not count, and the threshold is based on raw purchase price.

The report also cites buyer and practitioner complaints about owner replacement and addbacks. These are attention/problem signals, not evidence of demand for DealProof.

## Offer and GTM
- $0 CIM scorecard: addback risk, owner-replacement estimate, lender-realistic SDE range, and go/renegotiate/walk.
- $99 deep report per deal: lineage, benchmarks, EBITDA bridge, cash-proof flags.
- $99–$149/month searcher plan: unlimited screens, reports, pipeline, SOP alerts.
- $500–$2,000/month lender/broker API or white-label intake.
- First channels: Searchfunder and the ETA corner of X; daily evidence-led teardowns and partner pilots. Search terms are reportedly low-double-digit volume, while “quality of earnings” is about 1,300/mo at $18.36 CPC, so paid search is unattractive.

## Risks and constraints
Free/cheap incumbents can erase pricing. The direct regulatory trigger applies primarily at $3M+, searchers rarely close, credibility depends on CPA/SBA depth, false lender-realistic calls can destroy trust, and lender sales cycles are long. DealProof must remain a pre-screen, never present itself as an independent QoE or lender commitment.

## Build prompts extracted into product decisions
1. Build an explicit earnings bridge from CIM SDE to normalized cash flow.
2. Model loan needed, annual debt service, 1.25x DSCR, maximum supported loan, and equity gap.
3. Make each addback challengeable with evidence and replacement cost.
4. Output explainable GO / RENEGOTIATE / WALK calls with risk flags.
